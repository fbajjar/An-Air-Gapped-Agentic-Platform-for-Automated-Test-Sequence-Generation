# Comparator Design and Effort-Shift Quantification

Goal: quantify the claim that effort shifts from authoring to verification.

## Comparator conditions
1. **Agentic pipeline (current system)**  
   Measure authoring time, verification time, and total cycle time.
2. **Single-prompt condition**  
   One-shot prompt baseline with no multi-stage pipeline.
3. **Human authoring baseline**  
   Manual sequence creation without model assistance.

## Minimum metrics
- Authoring time (minutes)
- Verification/edit time (minutes)
- Total completion time (minutes)
- Final accepted step count
